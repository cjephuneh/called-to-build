The manifesto

What we believe

  1. Your work is not separate from your worship. Monday counts.
  2. Christ is the cornerstone. Everything else is built on Him or it won't stand.
  3. Profit is a tool, not a god. We build to serve, employ and give.
  4. Nobody builds alone. Builders need builders.
  5. We tell the truth about the build: the wins, the losses, the cracks.
  6. Africa is not waiting to be built for. We are called to build it.

Gatherings

Where builders meet

Monthly

Build Nights

An evening of worship, one founder's story and one word from Scripture, followed by open conversation. Come as you are and bring a builder.

Intimate · 12 seats

The Builders' Table

A dinner where a seasoned builder hosts twelve people around one honest question. No stage and no slides, just bread and truth.

Quarterly

Site Visits

We go inside a real business, whether a farm, a factory, a studio or a startup, and learn from the founder on their own ground.

Annual

Cornerstone Summit

The flagship. A full day of builders from across Africa with talks, workshops, worship and the year's commissioning.

theCalled to Build Podcast

The podcast

Real builders. Real talk.

Long, unhurried conversations with the people doing the work, whether they lead a pulpit, a payroll or a household. Every episode moves through three rooms.

  • FoundationWhat do you believe, and how did it hold when things broke?
  • BlueprintThe real numbers, decisions and mistakes behind the business.
  • ScaffoldingWho held you up? Family, friends, mentors and church.
